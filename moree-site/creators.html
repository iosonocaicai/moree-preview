Questions?

Who is this for?

People who enjoy making short videos and feel comfortable on camera. Show us your style through your video samples.

What would I make?

Short TikToks and Instagram Reels showing moree in everyday life. We provide scripts, references, and product demos to help you get started.

How much time does it take?

We're looking for at least three original videos a week. We'll agree on a weekly plan together based on your availability.

What are the collaboration details?

Compensation includes a base rate plus a performance bonus. Details will be discussed during the interview.

How do I apply?

Fill out our Google Form with your details and one or two video samples, then book a chat using the Calendly link inside.
